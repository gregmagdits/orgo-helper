Orgo Helper settings · lo-fi wireframes

One Settings window for the whole site, opened over the current page with the page dimmed behind it (a modal dialog, the same pattern as the Claude desktop app's Settings). Left column lists sections; the right side shows the selected section.

Structure: Molecules holds the molecule options every generator shares. Each generator then has its own section for settings only it needs, and can either follow the shared molecule options or override them. Laptop 1440 boards, plus a phone board at the end.
